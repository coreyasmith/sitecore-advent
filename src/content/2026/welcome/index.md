Welcome to the second Sitecore Community Advent Calendar! [Last year's Advent Calendar](/2025) was a lot of fun and I'm excited to be hosting it again this year.

![Waving Santa](./waving-santa.gif)

Each day from December 1 through December 25 I'll publish a new blog post on the site from a different member of the Sitecore community. This year we've got 25 new contributors with topics about [Sitecore Content Hub](https://www.sitecore.com/platform/digital-asset-management), [SitecoreAI](https://www.sitecore.com/platform) migrations, [Scrunch](https://www.sitecore.com/platform/ai-search), content optimization, platform sovereignty, and much more.

**A BIG THANKS** to each of the contributors for this year's Sitecore Community Advent Calendar [from the bottom of my heart](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N8NcQzMQN_U). This Advent Calendar would not be possible without each of the community members who took their time to write a post for this project. As you read through the calendar, please reach out to the authors and send them your thoughts on their post along with a thank you. Thank you 2026 contributors!!!

In the broader tech space, AI has, and will continue to, strain our web infrastructure and its security. While working on building [XM0 Templates for Sitecore Installation Framework](https://github.com/coreyasmith/sitecore-installation-framework-xm0-templates) this year, I observed that the Sitecore XM/XP cumulative and module hotfixes are including an increasing number of security fixes with each new version. It's important for Sitecore XM/XP customers to begin integrating these hotfixes into their CI/CD pipelines to stay ahead of security threats as best they can. [Extended Support for Sitecore 10.0 and 10.1 ends this month](https://support.sitecore.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0641167), so if you're on one of those versions, it's time to upgrade--no more security updates are coming after December 31.

2027 will certainly prove to be more exciting. AI models will continue to get more capable. The way we work will change even more. We will start working with Sitecore (SitecoreAI at least) less through its web interfaces (e.g., Page Builder) and more through text interfaces via MCP servers. The second [Global Virtual SUGCON will take place in January](https://virtual.sugcon.events/); [SUGCON Europe](https://europe.sugcon.events/) will take place in Alicante, Spain in April; and [SUGCON ANZ](https://anz.sugcon.events/) will take place in Melbourne, Australia in August.

But before we get too wrapped up with the year ahead, let's focus on the holiday season here and now. The Sitecore Community Advent Calendar has lots of great content to offer this year. Come back each day of the month for new insights about Sitecore and ways to make the most of this platform for your business, customers, or clients.

I wish the best to you and all of yours this holiday season. Merry Christmas, Happy Hanukkah, Happy Kwanzaa, and Happy Yule to you all! 🎅🎄
